🦋Butterfly Photonics Lab

How butterflies shape light

Three interactive models in one place: zoom into real wing nanostructures, see how Morpho-style ridges sort sunlight and stay cooler, and watch micro-pits trap light that a flat surface would throw away.

Model 1 · Structure

Nanophotonics Explorer

A 3D museum of five species, from the whole butterfly down to the nanostructure. Compare species side by side, toggle pigment vs. structural colour, and open the reflectance and biomimicry notes.

Model 2 · Colour & heat

Spectral & Thermal Heat Map

A black absorber vs. Morpho-inspired nanoridges above the same photovoltaic layer. Tune ridge spacing, sun angle and band to see scattering, electricity and temperature.

Model 3 · Light trapping

Ray-Tracing Light Trap

Flat glass vs. Papilio-style micro-cavities, traced ray by ray. Every extra bounce is another chance to absorb, so the pits capture more light, especially at low sun angles.

Nanostructure→Structural colour→Wavelength sorting & cooling→Light trapping

All three are simplified educational models, not full electromagnetic simulations. Each tab loads the first time you open it.
